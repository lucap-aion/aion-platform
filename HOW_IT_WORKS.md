# How AION Cover Works

A simple, non-technical walkthrough of the platform.

---

## What is AION Cover?

AION Cover is a **global protection service for luxury products**.

When someone buys a luxury item — a watch, a handbag, a piece of jewellery — the brand can offer them a digital "cover" through AION. The cover protects the product against things like theft, loss, or damage. If something happens, the customer files a claim directly through the platform and it gets resolved without paperwork or middlemen.

Think of it as **insurance built into the brand experience**: fully digital, and shared between three groups of people.

---

## The Three Portals

Everyone enters AION through the same website, but each type of user gets their own dedicated workspace.

### 1. The Customer Portal

For the end buyer — the person who bought the luxury product.

**What they can do**

- See all the covers attached to their products in one place
- View details for each cover: what's protected, until when, what's included
- File a new claim if something happens (theft, loss, damage)
- Upload photos and documents to support the claim
- Track the status of an open claim
- Manage their personal profile

**Their journey, in plain words**

1. They buy a luxury product from a partner brand.
2. They receive an email invitation to activate their digital cover.
3. They log in and see their product, neatly displayed with the cover info.
4. If something goes wrong, they open a claim in a few clicks. The brand and the AION team take it from there.

### 2. The Brand Portal

For the luxury brand — each partner (a watchmaker, a fashion house) gets its own workspace.

**What they can do**

- See all their customers and the covers issued
- Browse their product catalogue (the items eligible for cover)
- Issue new covers to customers
- Review and process incoming claims
- Manage their team (multiple people from the brand can have access)
- Adjust brand settings (logo, contact details, etc.)

**Their journey, in plain words**

1. The brand sells a product and issues a cover to the buyer.
2. The buyer is automatically invited to the platform.
3. When a claim comes in, the brand sees it in their dashboard.
4. They review the evidence and approve or escalate.

### 3. The Admin Portal

For the AION team — the internal control center.

**What they can do**

- Oversee all brands on the platform
- Manage the master product catalogue across all brands
- Review every customer, cover, and claim
- Onboard new brands and shop assistants
- Generate reports and view insights (registrations, claims volume, top categories)
- Manage other admins and access permissions

This is where the AION team makes sure the whole system runs smoothly.

## The Core Concepts

Four simple ideas power everything.

| Concept | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Brand | A luxury company that uses AION to offer protection on its products |
| Catalogue | The list of products that can be covered (a specific watch model, a handbag line) |
| Cover | A protection plan attached to one specific product owned by one specific customer |
| Claim | A request opened by a customer when something happens to a covered product |

A cover is always linked to **one customer + one product + one brand**. A claim is always linked to **one cover**.

---

## The End-to-End Flow

The full lifecycle, told as a story.

1. **A brand joins AION.** The AION team onboards them — sets up their brand page, catalogue, and team members.
2. **A customer buys a product**, either online or in a boutique.
3. **A cover is issued.** The brand (or a shop assistant) creates the cover in the system and links it to the customer's email.
4. **The customer is invited.** They receive an email, set up their account, and land on a dashboard showing their product and protection.
5. **Life happens.** Maybe nothing. Maybe a theft, a loss, an accidental damage.
6. **The customer opens a claim.** They pick the affected cover, describe what happened, and attach photos and documents.
7. **The brand reviews it.** They see the claim in their dashboard with all the evidence.
8. **The claim is resolved.** The customer is notified at every step by email.

---

## What Makes It Different

- **Brand-first design.** The product looks and feels luxurious — typography, colors, and spacing are tuned to feel premium.
- **One platform, three audiences.** Customers, brands, and the AION team each get an experience designed for what they need to do.
- **Digital-only, no paperwork.** Everything — issuing covers, filing claims, communicating — happens in-app. Email is used only for notifications.
- **Multi-brand by nature.** A customer who owns products from several partner brands sees them all in one unified portal.

---

## Where Notifications Fit In

Whenever something important happens — an account is created, a cover is issued, a claim moves forward — the system sends an email to the right person. Customers, brand teams, and admins all stay informed without having to check the platform constantly.
